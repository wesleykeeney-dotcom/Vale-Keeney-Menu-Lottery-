## Publication and QA contract

1. Match exact original source identity and archive its attribution, evidence reference and fingerprint. Distinguish original image verification from agreement with a secondary cookbook/PDF.
2. Compare **each** ingredient, numeric quantity, preparation detail, sequence, time, temperature and source-stated yield; explicitly record omissions/conflicts. Never fill gaps from culinary convention, summaries, images without method text, or AI guesses.
3. Preserve locked canonical originals. Any repaired transcription or household adaptation has its own named, versioned approval and source-status claim; corrections to governing originals require a separate reviewed PR.
4. A **planning shopping template** is not a cooking recipe. Partial/unmapped content stays marked, excluded from complete pantry recommendations, and never given generated directions.
5. Publish cooking content only when its exact recipe version has explicit authorization and a complete evidence-supported/independently approved method. Preserve 84 immutable lottery positions and do not infer household yield of five.
6. Derive recipe card, weekly Groceries, standalone **Shop this recipe** and Pantry from the exact approved ingredient version, with required vs optional and supply-only exclusions; optional alternatives require consistent named choices.
7. Ensure both scheduled and unscheduled grocery scopes, grocery checkmark isolation, manual additions, pantry persistence, storage and Android offline cache pass regression/acceptance testing.
8. No merge or production publication is authorized by this review document, any green CI, an ingredient-source match, or a household planning estimate. Owner approval remains mandatory.
